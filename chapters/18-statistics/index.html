18 Statistics¶

☐ For creators ☑ For visitors

Dynamic Web Extended includes a Statistics menu containing two pages:

  • Metrics —nothing is shown yet, it is reserved for future development.

  • Notable records — displays interesting genealogical records calculated from the family tree. It is based on the Gramps Records gramplet.

The Statistics menus are only displayed if the website creator has enables Statistics. See section 21.4 Include Statistics.

18.1 Notable records¶

Select Statistics → Notable records from the website menu.

The page identifies individuals and families associated with notable events and characteristics, such as:

  • Lifespans: People who died at the youngest or oldest ages.

  • Marriage: People who married or divorced at the youngest or oldest ages, and those with the most marriages.

  • Children: Individuals with the most children or grandchildren, and families with the most children.

The available results depend on the information recorded in the family tree. Some categories may have no qualifying records.

18.2 Viewing and selecting records¶

The page initially displays the selected records in each category, on the left. It displays the categories in a navigation menu on the right.

18.3 Number of records to show¶

Visitors can change the number displayed in each category using the Number to show field at the top of the page. Enter a whole number between 1 and 100, then press Enter or move to another field to update the display.

The new number is remembered when navigating between pages and when returning to the website using the same browser.

18.4 Selecting a category¶

The Jump to category navigation list at the side provides fast access to categories; click the browser back button to go back to the top.

When you select a category its category name is highlighted, making it easier to see that category.

Each result includes a linked individual or family name and the corresponding record value (such as number of children). Select a name to open its details page.

18.5 Ranking and tied records¶

Records are ordered by their values, with the highest or lowest values first, as appropriate for the category. Where records have equal values, they are listed alphabetically.

If several records are tied at the cutoff, all tied records are displayed, instead of leaving some of them out. Consequently, a category may show more results than the number selected.

18.6 Privacy and filtering¶

Notable records are calculated using the individuals and families included in the generated website.

The calculations respect the report's filtering, privacy and living-person settings. Consequently, the results may differ from records calculated using the complete Gramps family tree.

18.7 Interpreting the results¶

The records depend on the completeness and accuracy of the information entered in Gramps. For example, ages at marriage or parenthood require sufficient date information to calculate them.

The Person with most marriages category is new and counts distinct families with a Marriage or Alternate Marriage event. Multiple qualifying marriage events associated with the same family are counted as one marriage.

The results provide an interesting way to explore the family tree.
